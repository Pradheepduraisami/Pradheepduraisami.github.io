About Flantz

EVENTS THAT HIT DIFFERENT

FLANTZ is Chennai's go-to event management company specializing in college culturals. We've powered some of the city's most electrifying fests — from intimate department-level events to mega inter-college tournaments.

Our team understands the pulse of Tamil Nadu's college culture. We handle everything — concept, logistics, tech, artists, decor, and execution — so your student committee can focus on participating and winning.

Based in Chennai, we operate across Anna University affiliates, private deemed universities, engineering colleges, arts & science colleges, and law schools.
